## Coppervane

<ul class="reveal-list">
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Della Ashgrove</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>The miller's daughter. Rode into Coppervane at dawn looking for anyone willing to swing a weapon, and found you. Warm toward the band after Session 1. Went quiet in a very specific way when something called out in the Firwood on the ride home, quieter than her explanation accounted for.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Wendell Ashgrove</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Della's father, and the miller. Genuinely frightened of whatever was jamming his mill wheel, genuinely grateful once it wasn't a problem anymore. Paid what he had, eighteen copper pieces total, fed you well to make up the difference, and pointed you toward Coppervane's mayor for a better reward.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Cobb</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Works the mill. Wouldn't go near the wheel pit before you cleared it, and turned out to have good reason. Superstitious, and right.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Dunstan Wray</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Runs the Cracked Anvil, Coppervane's one real tavern, and doubles as the town's informal hiring contact. Half-elf, missing two fingers on his left hand, with a different story for how every time someone asks. Ran with a band himself for twenty-some years before settling down. Bought the round when you got back, and already has an opinion about what job suits you next.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Dinnion Werfer</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">named, not yet met</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Coppervane's mayor, at least on paper. You haven't met him yet, only heard the name from Wendell, who suggested he might be good for a better reward than a miller's savings.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Maren Coalgrave</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Runs one of the Firwood's logging crews. One of her loggers, Quinten Morton, went missing on her watch and was later confirmed dead. His effects are owed back to her.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Agnes Thistlewood</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>One of the regulars at the Stitch and Bitch Collective, a knot of Coppervane women who gossip, sew, and generally know everyone's business. Sharp-eyed and sharper-tongued. Has taken a noticeable, ongoing interest in Dimble since he first came to the Collective asking after work.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ul>

## The Firwood

<ul class="reveal-list">
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Wren Ashfoot</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>A Warden of the Old Growth, found the band in a quiet glade while they tracked the voice calling out from the trees. Warned them off a dangerous Waking rather than risk it, then fought alongside them anyway when they went in regardless. Identified the Firwood Caller outright as a leucrotta and explained how to actually track one.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The Firwood Caller</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">a leucrotta</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>The thing behind the voices in the trees since session one. Mimics the sound of the missing and the dead, including Quinten Morton's own voice calling for help well after his death. Fought directly twice now, wounded badly both times, and still alive, having fled north on each occasion.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Bee and Bramble</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">pixies, the Furrow Farm</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>The actual culprits behind the Furrow Farm's missing chickens. Not hostile once caught, happy to trade, and generous with what they'd stashed away.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Alden Furrow</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Owns the farm on the Firwood's edge where the chicken mystery played out. The band left without ever collecting payment or telling him what actually happened.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ul>

## A Bandit Camp, Deep in the Wood

<ul class="reveal-list">
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Jack O'Blades</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Leads a large, well-organized bandit company from a hidden camp somewhere deep in the Firwood. Never seen without a plain white mask, carries an unhurried, faintly wrong stillness that reads as unsettling before anyone can say why. Cold and mercurial, capable of mutilating someone mid-sentence with no real change in tone, and genuinely, visibly impressed by anyone who shows both strength and restraint. The band spared his champion rather than finish him, which earned a real offer to join his company. They declined, and now owe him a debt instead.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Boris Tuskbreaker</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Jack's undefeated champion, until the band fought and beat him. Spared rather than finished, which is what actually impressed Jack enough to make his offer.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The camp's seeress</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">unnamed</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>A blind woman the bandits keep close and treat better than any other captive. Jack trusts her reads on people completely. She's the one who suggested testing the band against Boris rather than simply selling them off, a choice that's steered events more than once.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Lennard</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>One of Jack's bandits. Lost a thumb to Jack's own blade for talking back during questioning, delivered with no ceremony at all.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ul>
