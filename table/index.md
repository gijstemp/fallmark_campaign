This came directly out of the pre-Session-Zero conversations we already had — this is what you told me you wanted, reflected back so we're all working from the same page.

<div class="callout">
<div class="callout-title">The Tone</div>
<ul>
<li><strong>No safety nets, no handrails, no chosen-one framing.</strong> You can end up in genuinely deadly situations without a built-in solution. Find one, or don't — either way, it stands.</li>
<li><strong>Enemies play to win.</strong> Real tactics, real stakes. Don't expect pulled punches by default.</li>
<li><strong>NPCs can be genuinely difficult.</strong> Aggressive, rude, unfair — not every NPC is likeable, and that's on purpose.</li>
<li><strong>Expect more descriptive environment and scene-setting</strong> than you might be used to. Places should feel distinct from each other.</li>
<li><strong>Combat, downtime, and character-driven moments</strong> are what this table wants most — expect prep to weight accordingly.</li>
</ul>
</div>

None of this is about making the game miserable. It's the opposite of softening — you asked for a world with real teeth, and that's what we're building.

## A Few House Rules

- **Secret rolls.** Sometimes I'll roll Perception, Insight, or similar checks for you, secretly, behind the screen — when whether you *failed* shouldn't be visible to you as a player. If your character would have no way of knowing they missed something, you shouldn't either.
- **A limiter on certain "safety net" spells.** Spells like *Goodberry* or *Tiny Hut* that would otherwise fully remove a survival pressure get a small built-in limiter, similar to how Guidance works. Exact details still to be confirmed at the table.
- **Excursion Resting.** We're using a specific variant resting system rather than vanilla short/long rests — worked well before, and we're keeping it. Full mechanics will be walked through at Session Zero.
- **Character creation.** A couple of specific things from character building last campaign are being dropped this time around — we'll talk through exactly what at Session Zero. What's staying: limited recovery resources, and the 2024 ruleset's weapon mastery properties.
- **Gunpowder exists, but only barely.** It's a very recent discovery, and so far it's only good for fireworks and controlled blasting — nobody's worked out how to weaponize it yet. That's world-flavor, not a house rule, but it affects what's plausible for a character to know or own.

## Coming Into Session Zero

Come with a character concept, not necessarily a finished character — mechanical details are easy to lock down once we're all in the room together. What's more useful to bring: a sense of who your character is, why they'd end up adventuring, and (if you already have thoughts) how they might connect to the other characters at the table.
