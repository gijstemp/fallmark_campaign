Aethermere is four hundred years removed from the night its smaller moon broke apart in the sky and rained down across the land. Everyone alive today grew up in the aftermath. This site is where that world lives, alongside your own story in it, updated as you actually play it.

<div class="callout">
<div class="callout-title">New to the table?</div>
Start with The World and The Fracturing. Everything else builds on those two.
</div>

<div class="callout">
<div class="callout-title">Already playing?</div>
Session Recaps has the story so far. The Band, People You've Met, Coppervane, and Mysteries track what your characters actually know, and grow after every session.
</div>
