No name yet. Four strangers a miller's daughter happened to pick up in one morning, now one session into whatever this turns into.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Dale Oakleaf</h3>
    <p>Human fighter, played by Tadas. Grew up working the Firwood's logging camps out of Coppervane, trained for the town's local militia on the side. Fights with a glaive and doesn't hesitate. Family legend holds that his grandfather was part of a band that killed a dragon, back when Coppervane could still tell that kind of story about itself.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Freddy Flash</h3>
    <p>Human paladin, Oath of Glory, played by Steinar. Full name Friedrich Klaus Brünhilde Wilhelm Flashheart, second-born son of a duke, cut off from the family fortune for choosing adventuring over the family business. Wants to become the hero from the stories he grew up reading, and to build a fortune that owes nothing to the one he gave up. Knows Kellen from an earlier stretch working out of Brinwatch.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Dimble</h3>
    <p>Deep gnome druid, Circle of Spores, played by Lloyd. Comes from a community still working through the grief of a disaster he's too young to actually remember, and carries that history a lot more lightly than most of his people would like. How he ended up this far from home, and why, hasn't come up yet.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Kellen Riordan</h3>
    <p>Human wizard, Conjuration, played by Þórður. Raised partly among a nomadic plains clan and partly at a small elven settlement that no longer exists, destroyed by raiders while he was away from it. He's been chasing leads on what happened to it, and to the people he lost there, ever since. Most recently worked as a hired wizard out of Brinwatch, where he met Freddy.</p>
  </div>
</div>
