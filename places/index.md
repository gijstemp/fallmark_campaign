Coppervane has its own page. This is everywhere else the band has actually set foot, or at least gotten close enough to.

## The Firwood

The forest Coppervane backs onto: logging trails, old growth, and more than a few stretches that stop making sense if you wander too far off the marked paths.

<ul class="reveal-list">
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The Furrow Farm</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>A farm on the Firwood's edge, run by a man named Alden Furrow. Twelve chickens went missing here over two weeks, which turned out to be the work of two pixies, Bee and Bramble, living in a small fey pocket hidden at the farm's old fence post. The chase ended peacefully, and the band spent a night inside the pocket itself, a place with its own stream and its own light, cut off cleanly at the border.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The logging trails</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Where Maren Coalgrave's crews work. One of her loggers, Quinten Morton, went missing overnight here, and the search for him is what first led the band deep enough into the Firwood to find real trouble.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The leucrotta's den</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Tracked by blood after a night fight in the fog, this is where the band confirmed Quinten Morton's death and later held their ground through a long night against a wolf pack and the wounded leucrotta both. The pack didn't survive it. The leucrotta did, badly hurt, and fled north. Strange boulders nearby, pouring fog, are still unexplained. See <a href="{{ '/mysteries/' | relative_url }}">Mysteries</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">A bandit camp, somewhere deep in the wood</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Hidden well off any trail the band could retrace on their own. Run by a masked bandit leader named Jack O'Blades, commanding a company large enough that fighting through it outright was never a realistic option. The band left owing him a standing debt instead, on his terms, collected whenever he decides the time's right.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ul>
