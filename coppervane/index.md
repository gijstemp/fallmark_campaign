Coppervane is a foothill trade town hard against the Firwood, where the forest gives way to the Ironpeak's western slopes. About fourteen hundred people live here: mostly human, with a real dwarven trade presence and a small, locally famous community of gnomish artificers. Most of the town works the Firwood's logging camps, runs the sawmill, or handles the caravan traffic moving ore and timber toward the mountain passes.

## What everyone in town already knows

Two centuries ago, a gnomish device meant for contained use went off in the open. Forty-seven people died, and the tunnel system underneath town took damage that's still argued about today. The site is fenced off at the top of the slope and has been for two hundred years. A memorial wall carries all forty-seven names, renewed every year on the anniversary. Ask anyone in town about the town's history and this is what they'll bring up first, usually within a minute.

That single day still shapes how the town treats its own gnomish neighbors. The artificers who stayed rather than leave for a bigger city are, whether they'd say so or not, still answering for something none of them did personally.

## Where you've actually been

The Cracked Anvil is the town's one real tavern and inn, run by a man named Dunstan Wray, and it doubles as the closest thing Coppervane has to a hiring hall. A corkboard by the door carries whatever work is going. Dunstan keeps a real, informed opinion about which jobs suit which people, and he'll say so.

Beyond that, you've mostly passed through: rooftops shrinking behind a departing cart, then the same rooftops again on the way back in. There's more town here than you've seen yet.

## Worth knowing

Coppervane sits within the Fellstone March, held by a family called House Varn, who by most accounts govern lightly and stay out of the way. The town's own mayor, a man named Dinnion Werfer, holds the official paperwork end of things. Most day-to-day disputes, though, actually go to Perrin Hallow, the blacksmith, who nobody elected to that job but everyone treats as if they had.
