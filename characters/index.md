This page will fill in further after Session Zero. For now, here's what's already settled.

## Starting Point

Your character is part of a newly formed band starting out in **Coppervane**, a small Central Plains trade town (see [The World]({{ '/world/' | relative_url }})). Levels 1-4 are about establishing who your character actually is before the stakes get complicated: small, personal, local jobs rather than world-shaking ones. Build a character who makes sense doing that kind of work.

## Ruleset Notes

- We're using the 2024 ("5.5e") **weapon mastery** properties.
- A few specific character-build choices from a previous campaign are intentionally being left out this time. The exact list is still being finalized and will be confirmed at Session Zero rather than guessed at here.
- Lasting wounds and exhaustion (see [Table Expectations]({{ '/table/' | relative_url }})) are staying in, rather than a full heal on every long rest.

## Race and Background

Any of the peoples described on the [Peoples of Aethermere]({{ '/peoples/' | relative_url }}) page are fair game. You don't need to justify your choice with a deep backstory. A character who's never thought about their culture's history is just as valid as one who carries it closely. If you want your background to tie into a specific region, faction, or piece of history, this is a great time to ask questions.

## Bringing It to the Table

The most useful thing to bring to Session Zero isn't a finished character sheet. It's a clear sense of:

- Who your character is and what they want.
- Why they'd be doing this kind of work at all.
- Whether you have any early ideas for how your character might know, or come to know, the rest of the band.

We'll build the mechanical details together once everyone's concepts are on the table. That's usually when the best ideas about the party's actual chemistry show up anyway.
