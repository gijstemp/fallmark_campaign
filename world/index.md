**Aethermere** is an ocean world built around one great continent, **Fallmark**, and a scattering of islands that orbit it as much culturally as geographically. Fallmark is large — large enough that crossing it corner to corner takes the better part of two months on foot, even on good roads. Nobody actually makes that trip. Real journeys follow the roads and rivers between the places that matter.

## The Sky

Look up at night and you'll see two moons.

The **Warden** is whole — pale, steady, completing its cycle the way a moon should. The **Broken Daughter** is not. It hangs in pieces, the largest fragments still holding something like their old shape, wrapped in a luminous debris field that glows faintly even on nights when the Warden is dark. Four hundred years ago it was whole too. Nobody currently alive remembers otherwise, and yet nobody has fully made peace with what they see overhead every night.

It is, by every account, beautiful. That's part of what nobody can quite settle with.

## The Regions of Fallmark

- **The Verdant Interior** — the western heart of the continent. An impossible, ancient-feeling forest grown up around a crater lake, dense enough to disorient anyone who isn't from there.
- **The Ironpeak Range** — the eastern spine. Dramatic, sky-splitting mountains, home to dwarven city-states built into the stone itself. Every trade route east or west goes through a pass somebody owns.
- **The Central Plains** — the agricultural heart of Fallmark, flat and river-crossed, the most populated and most politically tangled region on the continent.
- **The Northern Reaches** — wild, strange, and largely ungoverned. The least mapped, least predictable part of the world — and where a lot of adventuring work actually happens.
- **The Southern Coast** — rugged cliffs in the west, marsh delta in the east, a day's sail from the Deepwater Archipelago.
- **The Deepwater Archipelago** — eleven major islands and dozens of smaller ones, scattered across the sea beyond the Southern Coast. Half old land, half new — some islands are ancient coastline, others rose from the sea only four hundred years ago.

## Your Story Starts Small

You won't begin in a great capital city. Your band forms in **Coppervane**, a modest timber-and-trade town in the Central Plains, hard against the Ironpeak's western foothills. It's the kind of place with one real tavern, a handful of shops, and a population who all know each other's business — which means, for better or worse, they'll know yours too before long.

The wider world — its great cities, its powerful factions, its oldest mysteries — is out there waiting. You'll get to it. Everyone starts somewhere smaller.
