Gear with an actual effect, worth remembering you're carrying. Coin, mundane trinkets, and anything purely decorative don't make this list.

<ul class="reveal-list">
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">A sensory-link ribbon</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">held by Dimble</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Found in Bee and Bramble's stash at the Furrow Farm. Fey-made, no attunement needed. As an action, the wearer can share what they're currently seeing or hearing with one willing creature they can see, for up to a minute. Usable once per rest.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">A jar of pixie dust</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">held by Dale Oakleaf, 5 charges</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Traded fair and square with Bee and Bramble, in exchange for a hand-carved wooden pixie doll. As an action, a pinch sprinkled on a willing creature gives it a flying speed of 30 ft. for a minute. If they're still airborne when it ends, they float down instead of falling. Five pinches in the jar, no more once they're gone.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">A Pearl of Power</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">unclaimed, found at the leucrotta's den</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Looted off an unluckier spellcaster who never made it out of that stretch of the Firwood either. Requires attunement by a spellcaster. Once attuned, an action regains one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower. Usable once per dawn. Nobody's decided yet who's keeping it.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">Rootbreaker</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">held by Freddy Flash, bronze handaxe</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Pulled out of the wall in the ruin's flooded corridor and identified by Kellen. Green with age everywhere except the edge, which is still bright. A magic handaxe, no attunement needed, so its hits count as magical. Against Constructs and Plants it deals an extra 1d6 Radiant damage on a hit.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The Waypost Sealheart</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">held by Kellen Riordan, not yet attuned</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>A fist-sized black stone with color moving under its facets, always warm. Kellen took it from the dais the guardian was protecting. Without attunement it does nothing. Once attuned:</p>
      <p>You have Advantage on saves against being Restrained, Paralyzed or Petrified, and against anything that would banish, imprison or forcibly move you off your plane or location.</p>
      <p>As an action, at will, you can sense the rough direction and distance (to the nearest mile) of the nearest other place like the one it came from. Only that there's a place, that way. Nothing about what's there.</p>
      <p>Once every 30 days, as an action, you can target one creature you can see within 60 feet. It makes a DC 16 Charisma save. On a failure it's sealed in a shell of force for 1 hour, unable to act or be touched, until the shell is broken from outside (AC 18, 50 HP). On a success it takes 4d10 Force damage and is Restrained until the end of its next turn. Right after using this, you make a DC 15 Constitution save or gain a level of Exhaustion.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">The Waypost Lantern</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">held by Dale Oakleaf, not yet attuned</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>A small hooded lantern of dark metal, found still burning in the ruin's untouched side room. It needs no fuel and never smokes. Anyone can use it for bright light out to 30 feet and dim light 30 feet past that, and put it out or relight it as an action. Attuned and carrying it lit, you have Advantage on Survival checks to avoid getting lost in natural terrain, and the green-white flame slowly turns violet-blue within 500 feet of a fragment site or anywhere the Drift runs heavy.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="reveal-item" tabindex="0">
    <span class="reveal-name">A scroll of Protection from Poison</span>
    <span class="reveal-meta">in the dead man's pack</span>
    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Rolled in an oilskin tube, never used. Only Dimble or Freddy can read it. Ends one poison on a creature, and for an hour gives it Advantage on saves against being Poisoned and Resistance to Poison damage. Dimble used the band's other copy before the guardian fight.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ul>
