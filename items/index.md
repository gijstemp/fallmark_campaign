Gear with an actual effect, worth remembering you're carrying. Coin, mundane trinkets, and anything purely decorative don't make this list.

<ul class="reveal-list">

    <div class="reveal-popout">
      <p>Found in Bee and Bramble's stash at the Furrow Farm. Fey-made, no attunement needed. As an action, the wearer can share what they're currently seeing or hearing with one willing creature they can see, for up to a minute. Usable once per rest.</p>
    </div>
  </li>

As an action, a pinch sprinkled on a willing creature gives it a flying speed of 30 ft. for a minute. If they're still airborne when it ends, they float down instead of falling. Five pinches in the jar, no more once they're gone.</p>
    </div>
  </li>

    </div>
  </li>
</ul>
