<div class="callout-title">Right now</div>
It's the <strong>11th of Meltmonth, 400 AF</strong>. The band set out on the 8th, early spring, snow already melting and the roads clear. Three days of story time later, they've outrun a bandit camp, a leucrotta, and a wolf pack.
</div>

Fallmark runs on the **Common Reckoning**, a calendar a consortium of Central Plains city-states put together in the early post-Fracturing period so trade and governance would have one shared dating system. It's used almost everywhere now, though plenty of cultures keep their own traditional calendars alongside it for religious or cultural purposes.

The year has 360 days: twelve months, thirty days each. It drifts against the actual solar year by about five days a century, a flaw the consortium acknowledged, promised to fix, and never got around to. Astronomers mind. Nobody else really notices.

## The twelve months

<div class="card-grid">

<div class="card">
<h3>Ashmonth <span class="month-season">Late Winter</span></h3>
<p>The year begins here, on the 1st. Snow is still possible across the Central Plains, and the Ironpeak's upper passes stay closed.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Meltmonth <span class="month-season">Early Spring</span></h3>
<p>Snow melts, rivers run high, and travel resumes in earnest. <strong>Notable:</strong> Firstsail (15th), the Deepwater Archipelago's biggest celebration of the year, timed with the Archipelago League's annual council rotation.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Greenmonth <span class="month-season">Mid Spring</span></h3>
<p>The Verdant Interior turns visibly alive, and druidic communities everywhere hold their main observances. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Solstice of Green (20th).</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Clearmonth <span class="month-season">Late Spring</span></h3>
<p>The best month of the year for travel and adventuring work. Guild contract volume peaks. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Reunion Games, the king's own tournament, held over the last five days of the month in Vanthorpe.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Sunmonth <span class="month-season">Early Summer</span></h3>
<p>The hottest month, the longest days, the most monster activity all year. <strong>Notable:</strong> Fracturing Night (7th), the traditional date of the Broken Daughter's shattering. Restorers mark it with mourning. Inheritors mark it closer to a festival. Most people are honestly a little of both.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Dustmonth <span class="month-season">Mid Summer</span></h3>
<p>The driest month. River levels drop, but maritime travel is excellent. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Deepsong (third week), a week-long oral-tradition telling held by the western Deepwater islands, the closest thing the Archipelago has to a formal yearly assembly.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Harvestmonth <span class="month-season">Late Summer</span></h3>
<p>Harvest season across the Central Plains. Food is cheap, and every town has some version of a harvest festival. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Deep Seam Observance (12th), when dwarven priests make their deepest annual descent to bless the year's ore.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Goldmonth <span class="month-season">Early Autumn</span></h3>
<p>The Verdant Interior's canopy turns spectacular. A season that leans toward reflection. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Loom's Turning (15th), a day set aside for taking stock of the year so far.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Stormmonth <span class="month-season">Mid Autumn</span></h3>
<p>Storm season. Maritime travel turns dangerous and the Ironpeak passes start to close. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Ember's Observance (3rd), commemorating the first adventuring bands of the Fragment Wars.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Coldmonth <span class="month-season">Late Autumn</span></h3>
<p>The last month of genuinely reasonable travel. Winter preparations begin in earnest. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Long Night (20th), set aside across every culture in Aethermere for remembering the dead.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Deepmonth <span class="month-season">Early Winter</span></h3>
<p>The darkest month of the year. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Deepmind's Vigil (10th), observed with real seriousness in Greyveil, where the year's scholarship gets assessed.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Stillmonth <span class="month-season">Mid Winter</span></h3>
<p>The quietest month. Monster activity is at its lowest, and the world waits. <strong>Notable:</strong> the Balancing (15th), and the Year's End Silence on the 30th, the last day of the year, when no contracts get signed and no major decisions get made.</p>
</div>

## The moons

Look up on any clear night and you'll see two of them.

**The Warden** is whole, and keeps a steady thirty-day cycle that the Common Reckoning's own months were calibrated against. On the last night of each month, the 30th, the Warden disappears from the sky entirely. That's also the best night to get a clear look at the **Broken Daughter**, Aethermere's other moon, which doesn't cycle the way a moon should because it's no longer one object. It hangs in pieces, wrapped in a luminous debris field bright enough to glow on its own even when the Warden's gone dark.
