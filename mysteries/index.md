Not everything gets an answer the night you find it. This page is for the things that stuck, the moments where someone at the table went quiet, or where the world did something it wasn't supposed to. No answers here, just the questions as you'd actually ask them.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>The millrace that ran too far</h3>
    <p>Standing at Ashgrove Mill's sluice gate after the job was done, the water channel looked like it kept going, well past where the mill grounds actually stop. It was gone the instant anyone looked at it straight on, back the moment they looked away. Wendell saw nothing. Cobb saw nothing. Della saw nothing. Some of you did.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Something in the Firwood knows names</h3>
    <p>On the ride back from the mill, something called out from the trees. Not quite a voice, more the memory of one. Della went quiet in a way that told you more than what she actually said: that lumberjacks sometimes hear their own names out there, or a friend's voice, long after that friend stopped being anywhere to hear it from. She won't travel that road after dark.</p>
  </div>
</div>

This page grows as the band runs into more of these. If you've seen something the world doesn't have a good explanation for, it belongs here, whether or not anyone's found an answer yet.
