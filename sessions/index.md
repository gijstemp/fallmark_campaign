<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 1: what the millwheel woke</h3>
  <p class="session-date">1st Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>A miller's daughter named Della hired four strangers off the street to clear a jam at Ashgrove Mill. What should have been a rat problem turned into something else entirely: a creature that had been hiding in the debris the whole time, waiting for someone to disturb it. It didn't survive the encounter. Neither did the quiet ride home, once something in the Firwood called out from the trees and Della went pale enough to worry about.</p>
</div>

<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 2: what the fence post remembers</h3>
  <p class="session-date">2nd Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>Quinten Morton, one of Maren Coalgrave's loggers, went missing from camp overnight. His brother swore he heard something big moving nearby, then Quinten's own voice calling out from past the treeline. The search turned up an uprooted tree, a disturbed camp, and a strange mix of tracks leading toward the Furrow Farm, where a wasp nest turned out to be the more immediate problem.</p>
</div>

<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 3: borrowed feathers</h3>
  <p class="session-date">2nd-3rd Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>A stakeout at the Furrow Farm's henhouse revealed the real culprits behind the missing chickens: two pixies, Bee and Bramble, who turned out to be far more interested in trading than fighting. The band spent the night in a small fey pocket at the farm's old fence post. The next day, following a "wolves" lead the pixies pointed them toward, they were ambushed deep in the Firwood by a large, organized bandit company.</p>
</div>

<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 4: what the seeress whispered</h3>
  <p class="session-date">3rd Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>Marched into the bandit camp and interrogated, the band was made to prove themselves against the camp's undefeated champion, Boris Tuskbreaker, on the word of the camp's blind seeress. They won, and chose to spare him rather than finish the job, which impressed the camp's masked leader, Jack O'Blades, enough to offer them a place in his company. They declined, and left owing him a debt instead, to be collected on his own schedule.</p>
</div>

<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 5: what called from the trees</h3>
  <p class="session-date">3rd Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>Following the voice that had been calling out across the Firwood since session two, the band was found in a quiet glade by Wren Ashfoot, a Warden of the Old Growth, who warned them off a dangerous Waking rather than risk it. They fought it anyway. The creature behind the voice was confirmed as a leucrotta, a predator skilled at mimicking the sound of the missing and the dead.</p>
</div>

<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 6: what the fog remembers</h3>
  <p class="session-date">3rd-4th Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>On the road, a traveling herbalist named Jebridah temporarily restored sight to one of Freddy's blinded eyes, a fix that won't last the week, and offered an unconfirmed claim about who actually holds power near Willowmere. The band then turned back to hunt the leucrotta directly, fighting it overnight in a thickening fog before tracking it by blood to its den the next morning, where Quinten Morton's remains confirmed what had happened to him.</p>
</div>

<div class="session-entry">
  <h3>Session 7: what the wolves were running from</h3>
  <p class="session-date">4th Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>Digging in at the leucrotta's den for the night turned out to be the right call. A wolf pack and the wounded leucrotta both converged on the band's position at once. The pack didn't survive the fight. The leucrotta did, barely, and fled north. In a lull, a familiar scouting ahead found strange boulders pouring fog into the forest, a direction nobody can explain yet.</p>
</div>
