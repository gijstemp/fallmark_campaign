  <h3>Session 1: what the millwheel woke</h3>
  <p class="session-date">1st Ashmonth, 400 AF</p>
  <p>A miller's daughter named Della hired four strangers off the street to clear a jam at Ashgrove Mill. What should have been a rat problem turned into something else entirely: a creature that had been hiding in the debris the whole time, waiting for someone to disturb it. It didn't survive the encounter. Neither did the quiet ride home, once something in the Firwood called out from the trees and Della went pale enough to worry about.</p>
